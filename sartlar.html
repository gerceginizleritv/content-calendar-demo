2. What Shootboard is — and is not

Shootboard is a planning board for content. It keeps track of the posts that come out of a shoot: what each one is, when it goes out, and how far along it is.

It does not publish anything. It has no connection to Instagram, YouTube, TikTok or any other platform. Posting is something you do yourself, on the platform, at the time you chose. Shootboard only remembers the plan.

9. Deleting your account

Open My account in the app and use Delete your account. Your calendar, projects, ideas, scripts and settings are removed from the server at once, together with your files: the calendar-subscription file, any share link and your automatic backups. It cannot be undone, so export first if you want a copy. Backups roll over within 30 days.
