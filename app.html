      Deadline reminders

      Shootboard can tell you about a step before its deadline passes.

      These reminders are for project step deadlines — script, filming, audio, edit, approval, packaging, publishing. The posts on your publishing calendar are not included; you open Shootboard for those.

      When should it remind you?

      “When a step is already late” works in the browser notification only. A calendar file writes its reminders in advance, so it cannot know later that a step slipped.

      Your deadlines are published as a calendar address.

      This is the address you paste into your calendar:

      How do I add it?

      First press Copy address above. Then, in your calendar:

      • Google Calendar: Other calendars → Subscribe to calendar → From URL.
      • iPhone: Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Subscribed Calendar.
      • Outlook: Add calendar → Subscribe from web.

      Google Calendar does not fetch a new subscription right away — the first time, it can take a few hours to appear. That is Google's own schedule, not a mistake on your side.

      Calendars check the address on their own schedule.

      To remove it later you unsubscribe in your calendar app.

      A downloaded file enters your calendar as separate one-off events.
